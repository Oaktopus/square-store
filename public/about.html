The Oak & The Octopus

OAKTOPUS is the mix of an oak tree and an octopus — "oak" + "topus." The oak teaches us to stand firm; the octopus teaches us to adapt without breaking. We build gear for those who refuse to choose between strength and flexibility.

Born on October 10, 2024, in Anna, Texas. We source premium blank hats and shirts, add our own label, and finish each piece to order — including sublimated tumblers.

For the ones who stay true, and stay wild.
